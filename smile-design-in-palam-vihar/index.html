Cosmetic dentistry

Smile design in Palam Vihar, Gurugram

Smile design looks at your teeth, gums and face together and plans the right mix of treatments, from whitening to veneers, for a natural result.

What is smile design?

Rather than treating each tooth in isolation, smile design looks at tooth shape, colour, gum line, bite and facial proportions together.

The plan might include whitening, bonding, veneers, crowns or gum reshaping. We explain the steps, the order and the cost before starting.

Who is it for?

  • Uneven, chipped or discoloured teeth
  • A gummy or uneven smile
  • Preparing for a wedding or event
  • Anyone unhappy with how their smile looks

What happens, step by step

  1. Discussion of what you want to change
  2. Examination, photos and records
  3. A written treatment plan and cost
  4. Treatment in a sensible order
  5. Review and final adjustments

  • Keep up regular cleaning and check-ups

Cost of smile design in Gurugram

The cost depends on your mouth, so we give you a clear figure only after examining you. What usually affects it:

  • Treatments included
  • Number of teeth
  • Materials
  • Time over which treatment is done

Consultation is ₹400. If treatment is needed, we explain the options and the cost before we start, so there are no surprises.
