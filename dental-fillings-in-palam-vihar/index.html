What is dental fillings?

A cavity starts when bacteria and sugar soften the outer layers of a tooth. If it is caught early, the decayed part can be cleaned out and the space filled to restore the shape and function of the tooth.

Tooth-coloured (composite) fillings bond to the tooth and look natural, so they suit both front and back teeth in most cases.

Who is it for?

  • Visible dark spots or holes in a tooth
  • Sensitivity to sweets, hot or cold
  • Food catching in one spot
  • A small chip or crack
  • Worn or broken old fillings

What happens, step by step

  1. Check and, where needed, an X-ray
  2. Numbing if the cavity is deep
  3. Removing the decayed part of the tooth
  4. Placing the filling in layers and setting it with light
  5. Shaping and polishing so your bite feels natural

Benefits

Natural look

The shade is matched to your own tooth.

Conserves tooth

Only the damaged part is removed.

Same-day treatment

Most fillings are completed in a single visit.

Stops decay spreading

Treating early avoids a root canal later.

Aftercare
  • Wait until numbness fades before eating
  • Mild sensitivity for a few days can be normal
  • Avoid very hard food for the first day
  • Brush, floss and keep regular check-ups
  • Tell us if the bite feels high

Frequently asked questions

Does getting a filling hurt?

Numbing is used when needed, so you should feel pressure but not pain.

How long does a filling last?

It depends on size, location and how well you care for the tooth. Many last for years.

When is a filling not enough?

If decay has reached the nerve, a root canal may be needed. If much of the tooth is lost, a crown may be better.

Can old silver fillings be replaced?

Yes, if they are worn, leaking or decayed underneath. We will tell you whether replacement is actually needed.
