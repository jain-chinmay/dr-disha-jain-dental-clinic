What is kids' dentistry?

Baby teeth matter. They hold space for adult teeth, help children chew and speak, and decay in them can cause pain and infection. Early, regular visits catch problems while they are small.

A good first experience also builds trust, so children grow up comfortable with dental care rather than afraid of it.

Who is it for?

  • A child's first dental visit, ideally around the first birthday or when the first tooth appears
  • Dark spots or holes in baby teeth
  • Complaints of toothache or sensitivity
  • Thumb-sucking or other habits affecting the teeth
  • Regular six-monthly check-ups

What happens, step by step

  1. A gentle introduction and a chat with the child and parent
  2. A careful check of teeth, gums and bite
  3. Cleaning, and fluoride or sealants where suitable
  4. Treatment of any cavities, explained in child-friendly words
  5. Home-care advice for the parents, including brushing and diet

Benefits

Prevention first

Fluoride and sealants help protect teeth from decay.

Calm approach

Time is taken so children feel safe.

Protects adult teeth

Healthy baby teeth guide the permanent ones.

Parent guidance

Simple advice on brushing, snacks and habits.

Aftercare
  • Supervise brushing twice a day until about age 8
  • Use a pea-sized amount of fluoride toothpaste as advised
  • Limit sugary snacks and drinks between meals
  • Avoid bottle feeding at night after teeth appear
  • Keep regular check-up visits

Cost of kids' dentistry in Gurugram

  • The child's age and cooperation
  • Whether only a check-up or treatment is needed
  • Number of teeth treated
  • Preventive treatments such as sealants

Frequently asked questions

When should my child first see a dentist?

Around the first birthday, or when the first teeth come in. Early visits are usually short and about getting comfortable.

Do baby teeth really need fillings?

Yes, if they are decayed. Untreated decay can cause pain, infection and affect the adult teeth coming through.

My child is scared. What should I do?

Tell us beforehand. We go slowly, explain each step and never rush. A relaxed parent also helps.

Is fluoride safe for children?

In the right amount, yes. We will advise the correct toothpaste amount for your child's age.
