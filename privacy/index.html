Privacy

This website does not store your details. When you use the appointment form, your name, mobile number and request open in WhatsApp so you can send them to the clinic yourself.

If analytics or Google Ads measurement are enabled on this site, Google records usage such as pages viewed and taps on the Call, WhatsApp and appointment buttons, to help us improve the site and measure our advertising. Your name and phone number are not sent to Google by the website. No personal health information is collected through the website.

Messages you send us on WhatsApp or phone are used only to arrange and carry out your care. Questions about your information? Call us on +91 96506 47352.

General dental information on this site is not a substitute for a clinical examination.
