Gum care

Scaling and polishing in Palam Vihar, Gurugram

Scaling removes hardened tartar that brushing cannot, and polishing smooths and brightens the teeth. It is the simplest way to protect your gums.

What is scaling and polishing?

Plaque hardens into tartar within days. It irritates gums, causing bleeding, bad breath and, over time, gum disease.

A professional cleaning removes it and polishes away surface stains, leaving teeth smooth and fresh.

Who is it for?

  • Bleeding gums when brushing
  • Bad breath
  • Visible yellow or brown deposits
  • Routine check-up every six to twelve months

What happens, step by step

  1. Examination of teeth and gums
  2. Ultrasonic scaling to remove tartar
  3. Hand scaling where needed
  4. Polishing
  5. Advice on brushing and flossing

Benefits

Healthier gums

Reduces bleeding and inflammation.

Fresher breath

Removes the deposits that cause odour.

Brighter teeth

Surface stains are polished away.

Quick and comfortable

Usually under an hour.

Aftercare
  • Slight gum tenderness can last a day or two
  • Gentle brushing and flossing
  • Use a sensitivity toothpaste if teeth feel sensitive
  • Have it repeated as advised

Frequently asked questions

Does scaling damage the enamel?

No. Done properly, it removes deposits without harming the tooth.

How often should I have it?

Most people benefit from every six to twelve months. We will advise for you.

Why do my teeth feel sensitive afterwards?

Once tartar is removed, the surface is exposed. It usually settles in a few days.
