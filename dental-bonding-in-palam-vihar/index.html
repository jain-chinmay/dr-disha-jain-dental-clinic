Dental bonding in Palam Vihar, Gurugram

Bonding uses tooth-coloured resin shaped directly on the tooth to repair chips, close small gaps or improve shape, often in a single visit.

What is dental bonding?

The surface of the tooth is lightly prepared, resin is applied and sculpted, then set with a special light and polished.

It is a conservative treatment that removes little or no tooth. It suits small changes; larger changes may last longer with veneers or crowns.

Who is it for?

  • Small chips or cracks
  • Small gaps
  • Stained or uneven areas
  • Exposed root surfaces

What happens, step by step

  1. Choosing a matching shade
  2. Preparing the surface
  3. Applying and shaping the resin
  4. Hardening with light
  5. Final shaping and polish

Benefits

Single visit

Often completed in one appointment.

Little or no tooth reduction

Conservative option.

Affordable

Usually lower cost than veneers.

Easily repaired

Can be touched up if it chips.

Aftercare

  • Avoid biting nails or hard objects
  • Limit staining food and drinks early on
  • Brush, floss and have regular polishing
  • Report any rough edges

Frequently asked questions

How long does bonding last?

Typically some years, depending on habits and the area treated.

Does bonding stain?

Resin can stain over time more than porcelain, so cleaning and limiting stains helps.

Is it painful?

Usually no numbing is needed for small repairs.
