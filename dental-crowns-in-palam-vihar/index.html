Restorative dentistry

Dental crowns in Palam Vihar, Gurugram

A crown is a cap that covers a damaged tooth, restoring its shape, strength and appearance. It is often the final step after a root canal or when a tooth is too weak for a filling.

What is dental crowns?

When a tooth has a large filling, a crack, or has had a root canal, it can be too weak to chew on safely. A crown fits over the whole visible part of the tooth like a cap.

Crowns can be made of metal-supported porcelain, all-porcelain or zirconia. Dr. Jain will explain the options and which suits the tooth and your budget.

Who is it for?

  • A tooth with a large filling or little tooth left
  • A cracked or worn tooth
  • A tooth after root canal treatment
  • A badly shaped or discoloured tooth
  • A tooth that supports a bridge

What happens, step by step

  1. Examination and planning
  2. Shaping the tooth under numbing
  3. Taking an impression or scan, and fitting a temporary crown
  4. The crown is made in a dental laboratory
  5. Trying in and cementing the final crown
  6. Checking your bite and giving care advice

Benefits

Restores strength

Protects a weak tooth from breaking.

Natural appearance

Shade and shape are matched to neighbouring teeth.

Durable

With good care, crowns serve for many years.

Improves chewing

You can bite and chew with confidence again.

Aftercare
  • Avoid very sticky or hard food while the temporary crown is on
  • Brush and floss carefully around the crown
  • Avoid biting hard objects such as ice or pen caps
  • Keep regular check-ups
  • Contact us if the crown feels loose or high

Cost of dental crowns in Gurugram

The cost depends on your mouth, so we give you a clear figure only after examining you. What usually affects it:

  • Material chosen
  • Whether a root canal or build-up is needed first
  • Number of crowns
  • Laboratory work involved

Frequently asked questions

How many visits does a crown take?

Usually two: one to prepare the tooth and fit a temporary crown, and one to fit the final crown.

Which crown material is best?

It depends on the tooth. Zirconia and porcelain look more natural; metal-supported types are also used. We will explain the trade-offs.

Will it look natural?

Yes. The colour is matched to your other teeth.

How long does a crown last?

Many last for years if the tooth underneath stays healthy and you maintain good oral hygiene.
