What is clear aligners?

Each aligner is made to fit your teeth and is changed every couple of weeks, moving the teeth a little at a time. You wear them most of the day and take them out to eat and clean your teeth.

Aligners work well for mild to moderate crowding and gaps, but not every case. Dr. Jain will tell you honestly whether aligners or braces will give a better result.

Who is it for?

  • Mild to moderate crowding or spacing
  • Adults who want a discreet option
  • People who want to remove the appliance to eat and clean
  • Relapse after earlier orthodontic treatment

What happens, step by step

  1. Examination, X-rays and photographs or scans
  2. Treatment plan, timeline and cost explained
  3. Aligners made to fit your teeth
  4. Fitting and instructions on wearing and care
  5. Reviews every few weeks as you move to new trays
  6. A retainer to keep the result

Benefits

Discreet

Aftercare
  • Wear aligners for the number of hours advised each day
  • Remove them to eat and drink anything except water
  • Clean the aligners and brush your teeth before putting them back
  • Keep them in the case, not wrapped in a tissue
  • Wear your retainer after treatment

Frequently asked questions

Are aligners better than braces?

Neither is better for everyone. Aligners are more discreet and removable; braces can handle more complex cases. We will advise what suits you.

How many hours a day must I wear them?

Usually most of the day, around 20 to 22 hours. The results depend on wearing them consistently.

Will they be painful?

You may feel pressure for a few days with each new tray, which is normal.

How long does treatment take?

It depends on your teeth. Many cases take several months to a year or more.
