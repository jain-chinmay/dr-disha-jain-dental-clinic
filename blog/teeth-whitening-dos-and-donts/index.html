Teeth whitening: what to do and what to avoid

How to get the most from professional whitening and keep your results, including foods and habits to watch.

Before whitening

Have a check-up first. Cavities and gum problems should be treated before whitening, otherwise it can be uncomfortable. A professional clean also removes surface stains so the whitening works evenly.

Know what whitening can and cannot do

Whitening lightens natural teeth. Crowns, veneers and fillings do not change colour, so we plan around them.

After whitening

For the first two days, avoid tea, coffee, red wine, dark sauces and smoking, as the teeth are more prone to staining. Some sensitivity is normal and settles in a few days.

Keeping your result

Brush twice a day, rinse after dark drinks, and have regular cleanings. A touch-up may be useful after some time, depending on your habits.
