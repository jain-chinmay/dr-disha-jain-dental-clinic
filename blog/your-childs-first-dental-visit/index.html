Your child's first dental visit: what to expect

How to prepare your child for the first visit and what happens at the appointment.

When to go

Around the first birthday or when the first tooth appears. Early visits are short and mostly about getting comfortable.

How to prepare

Keep it positive and simple. Avoid words like hurt or needle. Tell your child the dentist will count and look at their teeth. A well-rested, fed child copes better, so pick a calm time of day.

What happens

We say hello, let your child see the chair and then gently look at the teeth and gums. If needed, we clean the teeth and talk to you about brushing, snacks and habits like thumb-sucking.

After the visit

Praise your child, whatever happened. If treatment is needed, we will explain it and plan visits at a pace that suits your child.
