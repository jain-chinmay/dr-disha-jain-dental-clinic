How to brush and floss for healthy gums

A simple routine for brushing and flossing that protects your gums and helps prevent bleeding and bad breath.

Brush twice a day, for two minutes

Use a soft brush and a fluoride toothpaste. Angle the bristles towards the gum line and use small, gentle circles. Scrubbing hard can damage gums and enamel.

Clean between your teeth every day

Brushing reaches only part of each tooth. Floss or interdental brushes clean the rest. Slide floss gently up and down each side of the tooth, curving it around the base.

Do not stop if your gums bleed

Bleeding gums are usually a sign of inflammation from plaque. Cleaning properly every day normally settles it within a couple of weeks. If bleeding continues, see a dentist.

Professional cleaning helps

Tartar cannot be brushed away. A scaling and polishing visit every six to twelve months removes it and lets us spot problems early.
