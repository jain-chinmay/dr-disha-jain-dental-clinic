Missing a tooth? Bridge, implant or denture

A plain-language comparison of the three main ways to replace a missing tooth, and what to ask your dentist.

Why replace it at all

A gap can let neighbouring teeth drift, make chewing harder and affect your smile. Replacing the tooth helps keep your bite balanced.

Dental bridge

A bridge anchors a replacement tooth to the teeth on either side. It is fixed in place and needs no surgery, but it uses the neighbouring teeth for support.

Dental implant

An implant is a post placed in the jaw that holds a crown. It stands alone and does not need the neighbouring teeth, but needs enough healthy bone and a few months of healing.

Denture

A denture is removable and can replace one or many teeth. It is a practical option when several teeth are missing, and can be combined with implants for extra stability.

How to choose

The right option depends on your bone, gums, the neighbouring teeth and your preferences. Dr. Jain will examine you and explain the pros and cons of each before you decide.
