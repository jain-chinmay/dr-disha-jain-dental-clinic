Restorative dentistry

Dental bridges in Palam Vihar, Gurugram

A dental bridge fills the gap left by a missing tooth, supported by the healthy teeth on either side. It restores chewing, speech and your smile without surgery.

What is dental bridges?

A bridge is a fixed row of teeth: a replacement tooth joined to crowns that sit on the teeth next to the gap. It stays in place and cannot be removed like a denture.

A bridge is one option. An implant is another, and Dr. Jain will explain which suits you based on the health of the neighbouring teeth and the bone.

Who is it for?

  • One or a few missing teeth next to healthy teeth
  • Those who prefer a fixed solution without surgery
  • People not suitable for an implant
  • Gaps affecting chewing or speech

What happens, step by step

  1. Examination, X-rays and planning
  2. Preparing the supporting teeth under numbing
  3. Impression and temporary bridge
  4. Laboratory fabrication
  5. Fitting and adjusting the final bridge

Benefits

Fixed in place

No removing or soaking, unlike a denture.

Restores chewing

Spreads biting force more evenly.

Improves appearance

Fills the visible gap naturally.

No surgery

Treatment is done on the teeth, not the bone.

Aftercare
  • Clean under the bridge daily with floss threaders or a water flosser
  • Keep regular check-ups
  • Avoid very hard food
  • Report any looseness early

Cost of dental bridges in Gurugram

The cost depends on your mouth, so we give you a clear figure only after examining you. What usually affects it:

  • Number of teeth being replaced
  • Material
  • Condition of the supporting teeth
  • Whether other treatment is needed first

Consultation is ₹400. If treatment is needed, we explain the options and the cost before we start, so there are no surprises.

Frequently asked questions

Is a bridge or an implant better?

Neither is best for everyone. A bridge needs no surgery but uses neighbouring teeth; an implant stands alone but needs enough bone. We will go through both.

How long does it take?

Usually two to three visits over a couple of weeks.

How do I clean a bridge?

Brush normally and clean underneath with floss threaders or interdental brushes. We will show you how.
