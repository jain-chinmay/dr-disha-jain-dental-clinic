Common questions

Is root canal treatment painful?

The tooth is numbed first, so it is usually similar to having a filling. Most people feel relief from the original pain afterwards.

How much is a consultation?

₹400. If treatment is needed, we explain the options and cost before we start.

Do I need an appointment?

Booking is best so you do not wait. In pain? Call us and we will try to fit you in.

Do you place dental implants?

Please call to discuss. Dr. Jain will advise whether an implant is right for you.
