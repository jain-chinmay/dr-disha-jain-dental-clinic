Dental implants

Dental implants in Palam Vihar, Gurugram

A dental implant is a small titanium post placed in the jaw to hold a crown, replacing a missing tooth from the root up. Dr. Jain gives honest advice on whether an implant suits you.

What is dental implants?

An implant acts like a tooth root. Once it has fused with the jawbone, a crown is fixed on top, so you chew and smile like with natural teeth.

Implants need enough healthy bone and gums, and good oral hygiene. Conditions such as uncontrolled diabetes and smoking affect healing, so we assess you carefully first.

Who is it for?

  • A missing tooth or teeth
  • People who do not want a removable denture
  • Those who prefer not to cut down neighbouring teeth for a bridge
  • Loose dentures needing more support

What happens, step by step

  1. Consultation, examination and X-rays or a scan
  2. Treatment plan and cost discussion
  3. Placing the implant under local anaesthesia
  4. Healing, usually a few months
  5. Fixing the abutment and crown
  6. Check-ups and maintenance

Cost of dental implants in Gurugram

The cost depends on your mouth, so we give you a clear figure only after examining you. What usually affects it:

  • Number of implants
  • Whether bone or gum work is needed first
  • Type of crown
  • Scans and X-rays needed

Consultation is ₹400. If treatment is needed, we explain the options and the cost before we start, so there are no surprises.

Frequently asked questions

Am I suitable for an implant?

It depends on bone, gum health and general health. An examination and X-ray will tell us.

Is the procedure painful?

It is done under local anaesthesia. Mild soreness afterwards is usual.

How long does it take?

Several months, because the implant needs time to fuse with the bone before the crown is fitted.

Implant or bridge?

Both are good options in the right case. We will explain the pros and cons for your mouth.
