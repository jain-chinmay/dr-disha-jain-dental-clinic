Cosmetic dentistry

Dental veneers in Palam Vihar, Gurugram

Veneers are thin, tooth-coloured shells bonded to the front of teeth to improve colour, shape and spacing. We plan them carefully so the result looks natural.

What is dental veneers?

A veneer covers the front surface of a tooth. Composite veneers are shaped directly on the tooth; porcelain veneers are made in a laboratory.

Veneers suit healthy teeth with cosmetic concerns. They are not the right answer for every smile, and Dr. Jain will explain simpler options when they would do.

Who is it for?

  • Chipped or slightly misshapen teeth
  • Small gaps between front teeth
  • Stains that whitening cannot remove
  • Uneven or worn front teeth

What happens, step by step

  1. Consultation and smile planning
  2. Preparing the teeth, usually by lightly reshaping the surface
  3. Impression or direct shaping
  4. Temporary or final veneers placed
  5. Checking colour, bite and polish

Benefits

Natural appearance

Shape and colour are matched to your face and teeth.

Corrects several concerns at once

Colour, shape and small gaps.

Stain-resistant

Particularly porcelain veneers.

Conservative options

Composite veneers need less tooth reduction.

Aftercare
  • Brush and floss as usual
  • Avoid biting hard objects
  • Wear a night guard if you grind your teeth
  • Regular check-ups and polishing

Cost of dental veneers in Gurugram

The cost depends on your mouth, so we give you a clear figure only after examining you. What usually affects it:

  • Material (composite or porcelain)
  • Number of teeth
  • Preparation needed
  • Laboratory work

Consultation is ₹400. If treatment is needed, we explain the options and the cost before we start, so there are no surprises.

Frequently asked questions

Are veneers permanent?

They are long-lasting but may need replacing eventually. Tooth reduction, when needed, is permanent.

Do veneers damage teeth?

Some surface enamel may be removed. We keep this to the minimum needed.

Veneer or crown?

Veneers cover the front; crowns cover the whole tooth. Crowns are better for weak or heavily filled teeth.
