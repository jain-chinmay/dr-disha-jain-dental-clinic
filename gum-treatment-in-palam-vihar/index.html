What is gum disease treatment?

Gingivitis is the early stage: gums are red, swollen and bleed easily. It is reversible with cleaning and good home care.

If untreated it can progress to periodontitis, where the supporting bone is damaged and teeth become loose. Treatment aims to control the infection and keep your teeth.

Who is it for?

  • Bleeding gums
  • Red or swollen gums
  • Persistent bad breath
  • Gums moving away from teeth
  • Teeth that feel loose

What happens, step by step

  1. Examination and measurement of gum pockets
  2. X-rays to check the bone
  3. Scaling and deep cleaning
  4. Home-care coaching
  5. Review and maintenance visits
  6. Advanced treatment if needed

Benefits

Saves teeth

Controls the problem that causes tooth loss.

Stops bleeding

Gums become firm and healthy.

Fresher breath

Aftercare

  • Brush twice daily and clean between teeth
  • Stop smoking
  • Come for regular maintenance cleanings
  • Control diabetes if you have it

Frequently asked questions

Why do my gums bleed?

Usually because of plaque and early gum disease. A cleaning and better brushing technique often fix it.

Can gum disease be cured?

Early gum disease can be reversed. Advanced disease can be controlled but needs lifelong maintenance.

Does it affect my overall health?

Gum disease has been linked with diabetes and heart conditions, so treating it matters.
